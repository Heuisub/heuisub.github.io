About me

Hello! I’m Heuisub Hyeon, a senior at Sejong University, majoring in Intelligent Mechatronics Engineering (Unmanned Vehicle Engineering track).

I’m interested in how humanoid robots and humans walk robustly in harsh environments, so that robots can someday work in places too dangerous for people.

  • Adaptive locomotion on uneven and extreme terrain (e.g., sand, mud, ice)
  • Balance control and recovery from external disturbances
  • Gait transitions across speeds (e.g., walk-to-run)
  • Blind locomotion relying on proprioception when vision is unavailable
  • Human-inspired locomotion
    • Optimality principles (e.g., minimizing energy, effort, or impact)
    • Developmental learning, from crawling to walking
